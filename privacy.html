CALORIETRACKER privacy

Last updated 27 September 2026

CalorieTracker is a private calorie and weight tracker used by one household. It isn't a public service and nobody else can sign up.

What it stores

  • Your food diary, body weight, daily targets and settings. These are private to your account.
  • The foods and dishes list, which is shared by the household.
  • If you connect a Fitbit: the total calories you burned each day (see below).

Everything is kept in a hosted database (Supabase) behind your login.

Google (Fitbit) data

Only if you choose Connect, CalorieTracker asks Google for read-only access to your activity and fitness data through the Google Health API. It reads one thing: the total calories you burned each day. That number is used only to show calories burned and your deficit or surplus inside the app.

  • It is stored with your private diary and shown only to you — not to the other person in the household.
  • It is never sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • The access tokens Google issues are kept on the server, where the app itself can't read them.
  • You can disconnect at any time in Settings (which also revokes access at Google), or from your Google Account under Security → Third-party connections.

CalorieTracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Barcodes

When you scan a product, its barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to look up the product. No personal details are sent with it.

Deleting your data

Disconnecting the Fitbit stops any new data being fetched. To have your data deleted, ask the app's owner using the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen.
